# Demo Script — 3–5 minutes

> Before recording: click **Reset demo** (top right). Confirm the green `live` badge (API key active). Stay in **Propose** mode.

## Beat 1 — The opportunity feed (0:00–0:40)
On **Next Best Actions**: "This is First Customers, an AI employee for a founder who just launched her candle brand on MadeThis. It already knows her one unfair advantage: the people who know her." Point at the top card (Jess, 94): every contact is scored **warmth × fit × timing** — visible bars, explainable math, and a "why now."

## Beat 2 — Propose → human decision (0:40–1:30)
Click **Jess Kane**. The agent drafts a personal text with reasoning, evidence chips, and expected effect. Read the draft aloud — it pivots from catch-up to pitch. "I know Jess. This is too salesy for how close we are." Click **Reject → "Too salesy for how close we are" → Reject & explain why**.

## Beat 3 — The observable learning cycle (1:30–2:30) ★ the money shot
Open the **Playbook** tab. Show the changelog: the rejection just wrote a new close-tier rule (before/after diff on screen). "Rejecting didn't just skip a message — it changed the agent's strategy." Back to **Next Best Actions**, click **Nina Alvarez** (also close tier). The new draft visibly obeys the rule — relationship first, soft ask — and the reasoning *cites the close-tier rules*. Same agent, one human correction, different behavior.

## Beat 4 — Outcome closes the loop (2:30–3:15)
Click **Approve & Send**. Delivered… typing… **💜 They bought it** — stats tick up ($28 revenue), and a "Learned just now" banner shows the agent reinforcing what worked. Optional: propose to **Tom Reyes** ("not really a candle person") — the agent leads with a **referral ask** instead; Tom refers Kayla. Open **Network**: Kayla appears as a new warm contact, "via Tom Reyes →". Relationship-based growth compounds.

## Beat 5 — Autopilot inside guardrails (3:15–4:15)
Toggle **Autopilot**. Show the armed banner: follow-ups only, to already-positive repliers, capped per day, warm/acquaintance tiers only — **close tier is locked**, first touches always come back to a human. Click **Run autopilot now**: it sends a follow-up (or defers with a logged reason). Open **Activity**: every action — agent, human, autopilot — with reasoning, outcome, and what was learned. Flip **Pause**: everything stops.

## Beat 6 — Close (4:15–4:45)
"Most GTM tools stop at a lead list. This owns the whole loop — finds the opportunity, asks permission, acts, watches the outcome, and *changes its own playbook*. For MadeThis, it's how 28,000 founders get their first revenue — from the relationships they already have."
